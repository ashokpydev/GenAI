ContextOps AI is an enterprise GenAI copilot for trusted knowledge retrieval, structured analytics, and controlled action planning.

Approved answers must be grounded in indexed company sources. If source context is missing, the assistant must say it does not know.

Sensitive operations such as ticket creation, email sending, and workflow execution require human approval before completion.

Every AI response must keep an audit trail with user, session, source citations, latency, confidence, and hallucination risk.
